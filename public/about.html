About this space

Not just a page. A platform that grows.

Xychen OS is a personal digital space for sharing projects, building useful browser tools, organizing resources, and trying out ideas.

Why it exists

This site brings different parts of my online work into one place. It should feel personal and polished, but also give visitors something useful to do not just something to scroll past.

The platform is being built gradually. Features will be added when they have a clear purpose and can work properly.

◈

Its identity

A personal digital platform with a distinct visual style and a flexible structure.

✧

Its principles

Useful over cluttered. Clear over complicated. Real features over placeholder buttons.

What belongs here?

Projects

Things I’m making, with honest descriptions and links to the source or demo.

Utilities

Small tools that work directly in the browser and make everyday tasks easier.

Experiments

Playful ideas and prototypes that can grow into something better over time.

This is an evolving personal project. Details and features will change as the platform develops.
