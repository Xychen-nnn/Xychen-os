Foundation

Built in small, working pieces.

The goal is to keep Xychen OS understandable and maintainable while the features grow.

Current direction

A multi-page personal platform with a shared design system, light/dark themes, project information, and browser-based utilities.

Design approach

Glass-inspired surfaces and jelly-like controls, with enough contrast and spacing to keep the interface readable on phones and desktops.

Implementation rule

Features should work as described, avoid unnecessary accounts or backend services, and be tested before being called complete.

Room to grow

More tools, writing, saved preferences, and interactive experiments can be added when their purpose is clear.
